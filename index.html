Our mission

Rigorous finance research for a world of new risks.

We combine empirical finance with frontier computational methods to help investors, firms, and policymakers measure, price, disclose, and manage emerging risks.

Bringing together faculty, students, and partners from finance, computing, cybersecurity, public health, and policy, we aim for research that is academically rigorous and directly useful in practice.

Research pillars

Three pillars, one shared toolkit

Our work is organized around two families of risk, with AI and quantum computing as a common methodological toolkit.

Pillar 1

ESG, Sustainability & Health

How climate, public health, and ESG factors shape asset prices, corporate behavior, and capital allocation.

  • Climate and physical risk pricing
  • Battery supply chain risk
  • PFAS and methane exposure
  • Pandemic and health-shock risk
  • ESG disclosure and greenwashing
Learn more
Pillar 2

Cyber & Digital Risk

How cyber threats affect firm value, governance, and market stability, and how prepared finance is for what comes next.

  • Cyber incidents and firm value
  • Cyber disclosure and insurance
  • Attacks on critical sectors like healthcare
  • Post-quantum readiness
Learn more
Pillar 3 · Cross-cutting

AI & Quantum for Finance

Frontier computational methods that serve as the lab's shared toolkit for measuring and managing risk.

  • Machine learning for risk measurement
  • LLMs for disclosure and text analysis
  • Quantum algorithms for portfolios
  • AI as cyber threat and defense

Why now

The risks that matter most are changing.

Traditional risk models were not built for these shocks. Finance needs new measures, new data, and new methods.

01

Climate and health shocks are market shocks

Heat, disasters, and pandemics move supply chains, labor markets, and asset prices.

02

Cyber risk is a board-level risk

Cyber incidents now carry disclosure obligations, governance scrutiny, and systemic consequences.

03

Technology is rewriting the toolkit

AI transforms how risk is measured, and quantum computing threatens the cryptography finance runs on.
