About the lab

Sustainable & Secure Finance: research on the emerging risks reshaping markets, firms, and policy.

Mission

Why we exist

The Frontier Risk Lab studies the emerging risks reshaping financial markets and corporate decision-making: climate change, public health, and sustainability; cyber threats; and the disruptions and opportunities created by artificial intelligence and quantum computing.

We combine rigorous empirical finance with frontier computational methods to help investors, firms, and policymakers measure, price, disclose, and manage these risks. By bringing together faculty, students, and partners from finance, computing, cybersecurity, public health, and policy, we aim to produce research that is both academically rigorous and directly useful to practice.

Our approach

How we work

Rigor first

Careful identification, transparent data, and replicable code. Every result should survive a skeptical referee.

Frontier methods

We bring machine learning, large language models, and quantum computing to problems traditional methods cannot reach.

Across disciplines

Finance, computing, cybersecurity, public health, and policy researchers working together with industry and government partners.

Why Mason

The right place for this work

George Mason University sits at the center of the Washington, D.C. region, close to financial regulators, federal agencies, and one of the country's densest concentrations of cybersecurity and technology organizations.

That proximity makes Mason a natural home for research connecting sustainability, health, cyber risk, and frontier technology to finance, and for training students who will manage these risks in practice.

The Frontier Risk Lab is based in the Costello College of Business and is proposed as a Mason Grand Challenge Initiative.
