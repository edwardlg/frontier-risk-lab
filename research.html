Pillar 1

ESG, Sustainability & Health

How climate change, public health, and broader ESG factors affect asset prices, corporate behavior, and capital allocation, from climate and pandemic risk and environmental hazards like PFAS and methane to battery supply chains and the credibility of sustainability disclosures.

Key questions

  • Do markets correctly price firms' exposure to climate, physical, and transition risk?
  • When are ESG and sustainability disclosures credible, and how can greenwashing be detected?
  • How do pandemics, heat, and pollution affect workforce health, productivity, and firm value?
  • How do battery supply chains, PFAS contamination, and methane emissions turn into financial risk for firms and investors?

Pillar 2

Cyber & Digital Risk

How cyber threats affect firm value, governance, and market stability; how firms disclose and insure cyber risk; and how prepared the financial system is for the post-quantum cryptography transition.

Key questions

  • How do markets react to cyber incidents, and what drives the size of the loss?
  • Do mandatory cyber disclosures improve pricing and governance, or create new costs?
  • How exposed is the financial system to quantum threats against today's encryption?

Pillar 3 · Cross-cutting

AI & Quantum for Finance

Frontier computational methods, from machine learning and large language models to quantum algorithms, that serve as the lab's shared toolkit for measuring and managing risk across both pillars.

Key questions

  • How can LLMs turn unstructured disclosures into reliable measures of risk?
  • Where do quantum and quantum-inspired methods beat classical ones on real finance problems?
  • How does AI change both the threat and the defense side of cyber risk?
